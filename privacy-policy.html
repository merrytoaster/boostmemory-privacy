Privacy Policy

Boost Memory: Kids (Android app com.ngidzyuk.boostmemory) · Effective date: October 26, 2026

Boost Memory: Kids is a memory game for children aged 4–8, made by ngidzyuk ("we"). We care about children's privacy. This policy explains what information is handled when the app is used.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no in-app purchases and no links to social networks for children.

Game progress (levels, stars, stickers, achievements and settings such as sound on/off) is saved only on the device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it; parents can also reset it in the app's “For Parents” section.

Voice prompts

Spoken hints and card names are pre-recorded audio files included in the app. If a phrase has no recording, the text-to-speech engine built into the device is used instead. Everything plays on the device; the app does not use the microphone and does not record or send any audio.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), an ad network certified for Google Play's Families program. Because the app is directed to children, we tell Unity that ads must be non-personalized (contextual): no behavioral or interest-based advertising and no ad tracking across apps.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Unity Ads SDK collects limited technical data, such as device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from the IP address, app diagnostics, and taps on the ads themselves. This data is processed by Unity under its own privacy policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Ads are shown only between rounds, never during play.

Children's privacy

We comply with Google Play's Families Policy and design the app in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Links that leave the app (this policy, contacting us) are placed behind a parental gate.

Permissions

The app uses Internet access only to load ads. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location services, photos or files.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
